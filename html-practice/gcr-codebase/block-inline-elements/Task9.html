Top 3 Travel Destinations

1. Manali

Manali is famous for mountains, snow, and beautiful scenery.

A peaceful destination for nature lovers.

2. Jaipur

Jaipur is known for forts, palaces, and colourful markets.

A wonderful place to explore history and culture.

3. Goa

Goa is popular for its beaches and coastal scenery.

A relaxing destination for a holiday by the sea.

Travel Tips

  • Check the weather before travelling.
  • Carry valid identification.
  • Book hotels in advance.
  • Keep the environment clean.
